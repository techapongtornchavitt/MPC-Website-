Product Overview

Sprockets & Pulley

Standard and custom machined sprockets and pulleys for every drive system

Roller Chain Sprockets Timing Pulleys Taper Bore Stainless Steel Custom Bore Multi-Strand

Motion Plus Corporation supplies a comprehensive range of sprockets and pulleys to match every chain and drive configuration. From standard single-strand roller chain sprockets to heavy-duty duplex and triplex variants, taper bore, and stainless steel options — all precision machined for smooth engagement and long service life.

Custom bore sizes, keyways, and tooth profiles are available on request. Contact our team to specify your exact requirements.

Standard & Custom Available Standard sprockets are available from stock. Custom bore sizes, keyways, hub lengths, and special tooth profiles can be manufactured to your specifications on request.
