About the Brand

About IWIS

IWIS (Internationale Wiesel) was founded in Munich in 1916. Over more than 100 years, the company has developed into one of the world's leading manufacturers of precision roller chains, conveyor chains, and timing drive systems. Headquartered in Germany with an international manufacturing and distribution network, IWIS serves industries across six continents.

IWIS holds ISO 606 certification across its product lines and maintains rigorous quality standards from raw material selection through to finished chain assembly and testing. Motion Plus Corporation is the authorised distributor of IWIS in Thailand and Southeast Asia, providing direct access to the full product range with local technical support and after-sales service.

Business Divisions

Our Divisions

Three Business Divisions

IWIS is organised around three focused divisions, each delivering specialist engineering capability for its target market segment.

Drive Systems

An exceptionally wide range of roller chains with attachments — outstanding quality and proven reliability across industrial applications worldwide. From standard roller chains to highly specialised custom designs, the Drive Systems division covers every requirement including maintenance-free and corrosion-resistant solutions. Innovative platforms such as the Chain Condition Monitoring System (CCM-S) and the VARIACTO Push-Pull Drive help customers make production processes efficient, safe, and free from unexpected downtime.

  • Standard and custom roller chains to ISO 606 and ANSI standards
  • Maintenance-free chains: MEGAlife, b.dry, and b.triton series
  • FLEXON specialist chains for complex conveying geometries
  • CCM-S Chain Condition Monitoring System for real-time wear detection
  • VARIACTO Push-Pull Drive for variable-ratio drive applications
  • Stainless steel and food-grade chain variants

Mobility Systems

With decades of expertise in timing drive systems for the automotive industry, IWIS Mobility Systems delivers forward-thinking solutions for both conventional powertrains and the rapidly growing field of e-mobility. Strong engineering and manufacturing capabilities make IWIS a reliable partner for customised system solutions and high-volume series production — from internal combustion engines through to full electric drivetrains.

  • Timing chain systems for passenger cars and commercial vehicles
  • E-mobility drive solutions for electric vehicles and e-bikes
  • OEM-specification engineering and validation
  • High-volume series production capability
  • Full coverage from ICE through to full EV drivetrains

Smart Connect

As a specialist in high-precision stamping and bending technology, IWIS Smart Connect develops sophisticated connection solutions for a wide range of industries — from battery connections for e-mobility and plug connectors for autonomous vehicle sensors to high-current contacts for alternative energy systems. All tooling is developed in-house, enabling tight quality control and rapid customisation.

  • Battery connections for e-mobility applications
  • Plug connectors for autonomous vehicle sensor systems
  • High-current contacts for alternative energy systems
  • In-house tooling development for full quality control
  • Harsh environment rated — designed for demanding conditions

Product Lines

Performance for Every Requirement

Specifically designated product lines ensure that technical requirements are met at every level — from ultra-premium precision for the most critical applications, to robust versatile performance for general industrial use. Each sub-brand carries its own engineering focus and quality commitment.

JWIS Premium

Maximum precision and length stability for the most demanding applications with tightest tolerances. Designed for high fatigue strength, peak performance, and maximum breaking load requirements.

ELITE High Quality

Versatile, reliable drive solutions for general industrial applications. High availability, flexibility, and a comprehensive product portfolio for dependable everyday performance.

FLEXON Specialist

Hinge chains, multiflex chains, and cast steel chains for complex conveying geometries. Engineered for applications that cannot be served by standard roller chain configurations.

MEGAlife

Long-life, maintenance-reduced chains for extended service intervals and lower total cost of ownership. Ideal for demanding environments where frequent maintenance is impractical.

b.dry & b.triton

Dry-running and corrosion-resistant systems for food, pharmaceutical, and clean environments. No lubrication required — designed for hygiene-critical conveying applications.

CCM-S Smart

Chain Condition Monitoring System for real-time wear detection and predictive maintenance. Reduces unplanned downtime and maximises chain service life through data-driven insight.
