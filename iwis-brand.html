Our Divisions

Three Pillars of IWIS Innovation

IWIS drive systems offers the right performance class for every requirement. With multiple strong brands, IWIS covers the entire spectrum of modern drive technology — from premium solutions with maximum precision to robust systems for versatile applications.

Drive Systems

An exceptionally wide range of roller chains with attachments — outstanding quality and proven reliability. From standard roller chains to highly specialised custom chains, covering every requirement including maintenance-free and corrosion-resistant designs. Innovative solutions such as the Chain Condition Monitoring System (CCM-S) and the VARIACTO Push-Pull Drive help customers make production processes efficient, safe and free from unexpected downtime.

Mobility Systems

With decades of expertise in timing drive systems for the automotive industry, IWIS delivers forward-thinking solutions for both conventional powertrains and the rapidly growing field of e-mobility. Strong engineering and manufacturing capabilities make IWIS a reliable partner for customised system solutions and high-volume series production in the automotive sector.

Smart Connect

As a specialist in high-precision stamping and bending technology, IWIS Smart Connect develops sophisticated connection solutions for a wide range of industries — from battery connections for e-mobility and plug connectors for autonomous vehicle sensors to high-current contacts for alternative energy systems. Manufactured with state-of-the-art tools developed in-house.

Product Brands

Performance for Every Requirement

Specifically designated product lines ensure that specific technical requirements are met — from ultra-premium precision to robust versatile performance.

JWIS Premium

Maximum precision, accuracy and length stability. Designed for demanding applications with tight tolerances and very high requirements for fatigue strength, performance and breaking load.

ELITE High Quality

High-quality, versatile drive solutions for almost any application. Reliability, high availability, flexibility and a comprehensive product portfolio for general industrial use.

FLEXON Specialist

Hinge chains, multiflex chains, cast steel chains and magnetic curves — engineered for complex conveying geometries and specialty handling applications.

MEGAlife

Long-life, maintenance-reduced chain solutions designed for extended service intervals and lower total cost of ownership in demanding environments.

b.dry & b.triton

Dry-running and corrosion-resistant chain systems for food, pharmaceutical, and clean-environment conveying applications where lubrication is not possible.

CCM-S Smart

Chain Condition Monitoring System — real-time wear detection for predictive maintenance, reducing unplanned downtime and maximising chain service life.
