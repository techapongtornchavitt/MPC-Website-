Product Overview

Palm Oil Industry Chains

Purpose-built chains for palm oil squeezing production lines

Hollow Pin Chain Solid Pin Chain Connecting Link P.4" Pitch P.6" Pitch Anti-Abrasion

The palm oil chain is developed based on our applied research on palm oil squeezing production lines. During the production process, under the severe control of reasonable selection of materials, precision finishing, special heat treatment, precision assembly and pre-oiling, the consistency of dimensions make sure that the chain and chain wheel mesh with each other better, and the long-lasting anti abrasive characteristics underline the products better quality.

Engineered for palm oil squeezing lines — precision assembly, special heat treatment, and pre-oiling ensure consistent meshing with chain wheels and long-lasting anti-abrasive performance.
ISO9001 Quality Assurance Every link in the production process — material selection, precision finishing, heat treatment, assembly, and pre-oiling — is controlled under the ISO9001 quality assurance system.
