Chain Solutions Built for the Harshest Steel Environments

Steel manufacturing pushes industrial chains to their absolute limits. From blast furnace charge conveyors running at extreme temperatures to rolling mill drive chains under constant high-torque loads, every chain in a steel plant must perform flawlessly — because downtime is unacceptable.

Motion Plus Corporation supplies authorised, certified chain solutions specifically selected and tested for steel industry applications. Our technical team works with your maintenance engineers to specify chains that match your exact operating conditions — pitch, breaking load, temperature rating, and surface treatment.

We distribute chains from IWIS (Germany) and HS Chain (Korea) — two of the world's most trusted names in heavy industrial chain manufacturing — plus our proprietary TRIO Chain for demanding conveyor applications.

Lubrication depends on the operating temperature zone. For ambient-temperature conveyors, we recommend high-pressure EP chain oils applied via automatic lubrication systems.
