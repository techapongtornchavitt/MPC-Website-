Common Questions About Our Chain Solutions

Everything you need to know about MPC's products, services, and support.

MPC specialises in industrial chain solutions for four key sectors: steel manufacturing, cement production, palm oil milling, and sugar processing. We also serve escalator maintenance companies, animal feed mills, automotive manufacturing, and general industrial facilities across Thailand and Southeast Asia.
MPC is the authorised distributor for several world-class brands:
  • IWIS (Germany) — precision engineering chains & systems
  • HS Chain (Korea) — heavy conveyor & industrial chains
  • KMH (Japan) — pipe conveyor systems & components
  • Dongbo (Korea) — ANSI-certified standard roller chains
We also offer our proprietary UNO Chain (ISO roller chains) and TRIO Chain (heavy forged chains) house brands.
Yes — after-sales service is a core part of MPC's offering. We provide technical consultation, chain failure analysis, and direct coordination with manufacturers to resolve issues quickly. Unlike pure distributors, we handle warranty claims and technical problems on your behalf, so you don't need to deal with overseas manufacturers directly.
We deliver across all of Thailand, with express logistics from our Bangkok warehouse. We also serve customers throughout Southeast Asia including Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam, and the broader ASEAN region. Contact us for export pricing and lead times.
Our technical team selects the right chain based on your application parameters: load capacity, operating speed, temperature range, chemical exposure, and lubrication conditions. Share your existing chain number, equipment model, or operating conditions with us and we'll recommend the optimal solution — free of charge.
MPC supplies chains to international standards including ANSI B29.1, ISO 606, DIN 8187/8188, and JIS B1801. Our range covers pitches from 6.35mm (1/4") up to 102mm heavy forged chains, in single, double, and triple strand configurations. We also supply metric and non-standard chains on request.
