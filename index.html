Our Divisions

Drive Systems

Our drive and conveyor chains offer an exceptionally wide range of roller chains with attachments, outstanding quality and proven reliability. From standard roller chains to highly specialised custom chains — including maintenance-free and corrosion-resistant designs. Innovative solutions such as our Chain Condition Monitoring System and the VARIACTO Push-Pull Drive help customers make production processes efficient, safe and free from unexpected downtime.

Mobility Systems

With decades of expertise in timing drive systems for the automotive industry, we deliver forward-thinking solutions for both conventional powertrains and the rapidly growing field of e-mobility. Our strong engineering and manufacturing capabilities make us a reliable partner for customised system solutions and high-volume series production in the automotive sector.

Smart Connect

As a specialist in high-precision stamping and bending technology, iwis Smart Connect develops sophisticated connection solutions across a wide range of industries — from battery connections for e-mobility and plug connectors for autonomous vehicle sensors to high-current contacts for alternative energy systems.
