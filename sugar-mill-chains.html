Process Coverage

Full Sugar Mill Chain Solutions

From raw sugarcane intake to packed finished product — purpose-built chains engineered for every stage of modern sugar production worldwide.

01
Raw Materials Handling
Heavy-duty carrier chains for transporting raw sugarcane and beet from intake to processing — designed for continuous high-load operation with minimum downtime.
02
Juicing Process
Drive and transmission chains engineered for juice extraction equipment — delivering precise torque transfer and long service life in high-moisture environments.
03
Bagasse Conveying
Specialised bagasse feeder chains (SS2284 P.9", SS800 P.8") for efficient biomass handling and fuel conveying — built to resist the abrasive nature of sugarcane fibre.
04
Settling & Evaporation
Heat-resistant conveying chains for juice clarification, settling and evaporation stages — maintaining dimensional stability under sustained thermal exposure.
05
Drying & Screening
Precision chains for sugar drying, grading and screening operations — maintaining consistent pitch accuracy to ensure uniform product quality at every batch.
06
Packing Line
Reliable conveyor and indexing chains for sugar packing and bagging lines — engineered for smooth, repeatable motion in food-adjacent production environments.
