Product Overview

Small Pitch Conveyor Chain

ANSI KS B 1407 ISO 606 45+ Yrs Korea 1 / 2 / 3 Strand

Small Pitch Conveyor Chain are used in a wide variety of applications such as packaging, food, cosmetics, pharmaceutical, manufacturing and many other industrial applications. These chains are manufactured based on the components of standard roller chains and specially designed attachments or parts suitable for conveying applications.

  • All standard and wide contour attachments are available in a wide range of sizes.
  • A wide variety of assembled chain and components are stocked for quick delivery service.
  • Raw materials used are high quality carbon steels, stainless steels, engineering plastic, with protective coatings (nickel, zinc, chromium plating and dacrotized coating).
  • Other special attachment can be supplied against special order.

Dong Bo, as a leader in Korean chain manufacturing with over 45 years of experience, can help you to find the best solution for your conveyor system.
