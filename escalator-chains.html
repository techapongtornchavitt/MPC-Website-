House Brand

Escalator Chains

Step chains, handrail drive chains and drive chains for commercial and public-transport escalators and moving walkways.

Motion Plus Corporation supplies escalator chains in Thailand under the MPC house brand, covering step chains, auto passage (moving walkway) chains, handrail driving chains and escalator drive chains. The range fits commercial escalators in shopping malls and office buildings as well as heavy-duty escalators in metro stations and airports.

Every chain listed below shows its pitch, roller diameter, pin diameter and tensile strength, so you can match a replacement to your escalator quickly. Not sure which chain you need? Send us the chain number, a photo of the old chain or the escalator model, and our team will help you identify the right part and prepare a quotation.
