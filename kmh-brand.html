About KMH

Precision Pipe Systems from Germany

KMH (Kunststoff- und Metallverarbeitung Heinsberg GmbH) is a German manufacturer of pipe conveyor systems, founded over 35 years ago. The company specialises in the engineering and production of both standard modular and fully custom-designed pipe conveyor systems for bulk material handling across a wide range of industries.

KMH's product range combines a large standard modular system with the capability to engineer bespoke solutions to exact customer specifications. With over 5,000 product variants, an international agency network, and a team of experienced engineers, KMH delivers the certainty of the optimal pipe system for each application.

Our Engineering Approach

Our Engineering Approach

Modular Standard Range

KMH's modular standard range covers an extensive variety of pipe systems, components, and accessories. The standardised design allows for rapid selection, short lead times, and easy integration into existing conveyor infrastructure.

  • Wide variety of standard pipe sizes and wall thicknesses
  • Pre-engineered connector systems and accessories
  • Broad compatibility with existing conveyor systems
  • Fast delivery from stock
  • Comprehensive technical documentation and selection guides

Custom-Made Production

For applications requiring non-standard specifications, KMH engineers and manufactures fully bespoke pipe systems to customer requirements.

  • Customer-specific dimensions, materials, and surface treatments
  • Full engineering support from specification to delivery
  • Manufactured to tight tolerances for precise fit
  • Suitable for high-pressure, corrosive, or extreme-temperature environments
  • CE-marked and compliant with relevant industry standards

Speed & Flexibility

KMH's production infrastructure and experienced team allow for both rapid standard deliveries and flexible accommodation of special engineering requests.

  • Short lead times for standard products
  • Dedicated project management for custom orders
  • Scalable production for low and high volumes
  • Direct technical liaison with engineering team
  • After-sales support and replacement part availability

Our Philosophy

Engineered for Certainty

"With certainty — the optimal pipe system for your application."

KMH's engineering philosophy centres on providing complete confidence in the solution delivered. Every pipe system — whether selected from the standard range or engineered to bespoke specifications — is designed to meet exact application requirements and perform reliably throughout its service life.
