The KMH Philosophy

Speed. Stock. Speciality.

KMH concentrates on the essentials — the modular products customers need from one day to the next. Delivery ex-works. Full speed. KMH has (almost) the whole range in stock at all times. All that needs to be decided is the surface finish: powder-coated, pickled, blasted, galvanised, and more.

"With certainty — the optimal pipe system for your application."

Individual production is part of the KMH philosophy. Even the most complicated special products are built within the shortest time after technical clarification. Custom-made special products of all kinds are KMH's speciality. KMH is the No. 1 in special construction.

What Sets KMH Apart

Three Strengths, One System

Modular Standard Range

KMH's modular system includes thousands of perfectly coordinated standard products. It is flexible and easy to manage — select the required components, interconnect them, and complex installations can be implemented quickly and easily. The entire standard range is held in stock for immediate ex-works delivery.

Custom Special Production

Where the standard system is not enough, KMH has "SPECIALIZED" in individual solutions. Modified standard products from the catalogue, or entirely new developments — KMH special productions are manufactured according to your exact specifications and requirements, and are compatible with the standard system.

Speed & Flexibility

KMH is fast, innovative and competent in implementation. For over 35 years, KMH has delivered worldwide — whether standard range, series, or individual production. KMH responds quickly and flexibly to special requirements, with all manufacturing entirely in-house. Made in Germany.
