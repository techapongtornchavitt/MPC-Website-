Chains Engineered for the Wet, Acidic Palm Oil Mill Environment

We have developed chains specifically for palm oil industry that require special characteristics to withstand the acidic and wet environment of palm oil mill, which can be the cause of the premature failure and breakdown of conventional chains.

The anti-corrosion chain is specially developed and tested to be used in the severe conditions of palm oil mills and provides long service life and reduce the downtime, resulting in higher efficiency and lower operating cost.

Palm Oil Chain — Frequently Asked Questions

Palm oil sterilizer conveyors require chains that can withstand saturated steam at 140–145°C and exposure to fatty acid condensate. Specialist grades with alloy steel components, heat-resistant seals (where applicable), and appropriate high-temperature, water-resistant lubrication are specified. The chain must also handle the weight of loaded sterilizer trolleys. MPC engineers specify the correct grade for your sterilizer system.

For product-contact zones in palm oil processing — including fruit conveyors, clarification systems, and oil handling lines — we supply chains with food-safe H1 lubricants, FDA-compliant materials, stainless steel components, and nickel-plated finishes. These meet the hygiene standards required for RSPO certification, BRC, SQF, and other food industry audits.
Replacement intervals depend on chain quality, lubrication frequency, and the specific process environment. In sterilizer applications, even high-quality chains may need replacement every 12–24 months due to the severe environment. In more protected zones with good lubrication, 3–5 year service life is achievable. We recommend 3% elongation as the replacement trigger — beyond this, sprocket wear and chain jumping become risks.
