Our Story

Built on Precision,
Driven by Excellence

Motion Plus Corporation was founded with a singular mission: to bring world-class industrial chain technology to the rapidly growing manufacturing and processing sectors of Southeast Asia.

As an authorised distributor for six internationally recognised chain brands, MPC provides more than just products — we deliver precision-engineered solutions, reliable after-sales support, and long-term partnerships that keep our clients' operations running at peak performance.

Our team works alongside clients from product delivery through to ongoing maintenance and support, ensuring every chain solution is precisely matched to its application.
