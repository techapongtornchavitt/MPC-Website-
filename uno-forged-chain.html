Product Overview

Forged Chain UNO

Pitch 142 mm — Large-Pitch Heavy-Duty Conveyor Chain

Top Quality Steel High Temp Forged Heat Treated Pin & Clip Lock Q.C. Inspected

Top quality forged chain with its own unique characteristics and designed suitably for all kinds of conveyor systems, particularly with heavy duty industries, such as steel mill, cement plant, animal feed mill.

Forged Chain TRIO (pitch 102 mm.) and Forged Chain UNO (pitch 142 mm.) simply able to handle well with high load capacity as well as wear & tear endurance against rail guide operation, high temperature, and dust environment.

Forged Chain TRIO & UNO produced from high quality steel with high temp forging process equipment, along with proper heat treatment in order to sustain high standardization, to create higher strength at surface but flexibility during performance, ending with machining process of chain links. The chain product perfectly come with pin and clip locks for easy access.

Every single finished goods process must be inspected under Q.C. (Quality Control) to assure for the best quality.

Forged Chain TRIO & UNO can ensure you with superb quality for smooth operation.
